# Channel controls

Open your room’s chat and look for the buttons or dropdown. Server admins can [turn individual controls on and off](server-settings.md#control-panel).

## Room controls

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Public, Lock, Hide | Public lets the [selected role](getting-started.md#creator-settings) see and join the room. Lock stops them joining. Hide stops them seeing it too. |
| Rename | Type a new name or leave it blank to go back to the template. The server’s [profanity filter](server-settings.md#profanity-filter) checks custom names. |
| User limit | Change how many people can join. |
| Ban, Allow | Block or let in a member or role. Banning someone also disconnects them from the room. |
| Mute, Deafen | Mute, deafen, unmute or undeafen someone in the room. |
| Clear messages | Delete chat messages and keep the panel. |
| Delete channel | Confirm to delete the room. |

## Ownership

Use **Give ownership** to hand the room to someone who’s in it or give it up. Once the owner leaves or gives it up, the next person in the room to use the panel can claim it.

## Right-click actions

Right-click a member in Discord and choose **Apps**. If you own the room or can claim it, you can ban, allow, mute or deafen them from that menu. **Unmute & Undeafen User** clears both at once.
